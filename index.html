My Mission

My mission is to uncover the natural order of things: to see beyond appearances, recognize the desires people try to hide, and draw them into the light. Some are born to command, others to surrender. I find the tension between them irresistible—and I enjoy giving it a little push.

One sharp strike of my heel against the floor is all it takes: a beta pledges absolute submission, an alpha burns with desire, and a woman weeps for mercy.

I pour that fascination into my stories, where power shifts, temptation whispers, and every character discovers just how far they are willing to go. Literature is only the beginning: soon, I hope to bring these worlds to life through other media, including video games.
